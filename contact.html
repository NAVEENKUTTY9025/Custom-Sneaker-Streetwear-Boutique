Before You Hit Send

Common questions we get — might save you a message.

How long does a custom order take?

Standard turnaround is 3–7 days depending on the package. Elite orders can be done in 24–48 hours. Shipping adds 2–5 days in the US.

Can I mail in my own sneakers?

Yes! We accept shipped-in pairs for all custom packages. Just mention it in your message and we'll send you a prepaid label (US only).

Do you ship internationally?

We ship worldwide. Elite packages include free international shipping. Rates for other tiers are calculated at checkout.

How do I start a custom order?

Fill out the form above with topic "Custom Sneaker Order" and include your size, shoe model, and any design ideas. We'll reply with a quote and timeline.

Do you do bulk or team orders?

Absolutely. We handle team customs, brand activations, and bulk gifting. Reach out with "Collaboration" as your topic and we'll put together a custom quote.

What if I need to change my order?

Reach out ASAP. If we haven't started painting yet, changes are easy. Once paint is on, changes depend on the design — we'll always work with you.

Don't want to wait?

Skip the form and start your custom order directly. 10% off your first pair with code SOLE10.
