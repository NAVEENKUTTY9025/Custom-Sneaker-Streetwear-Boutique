Shipping
Yes, we ship worldwide. Elite packages include free international shipping. For other tiers, rates are calculated at checkout based on your location and package weight.
US shipping starts at $9.95 for standard (3–5 days) and $19.95 for express (1–2 days). International shipping starts at $24.95. Elite customers ship free worldwide.
Returns & Exchanges
Custom-painted sneakers are non-returnable (they're one-of-one and made just for you). Apparel and unworn, non-custom items can be returned within 30 days of delivery in original condition. See our full Returns Policy.
Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue. If it's our mistake, we'll repaint, replace, or refund — your choice. Your satisfaction is non-negotiable.
