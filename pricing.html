FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know before you order.

Can I send in my own sneakers?

Absolutely. We accept shipped-in pairs for all packages. Just select "Send My Pair" at checkout and we'll email you a prepaid shipping label (US only).

How does the membership work?

Membership gives you 20% off every custom order, free touch-up kits, and early access to limited drops. You can cancel anytime — no long-term commitment.

Are the paints waterproof?

Yes. We use professional-grade leather paints and sealants designed for footwear. They're water-resistant and built to flex without cracking.

What if I don't like the design?

Every order includes a digital proof before painting starts. Creator and Elite packages include unlimited revisions, so we keep tweaking until you love it.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Elite packages include free international shipping. Other tiers have calculated rates at checkout.

How long does a custom take?

Starter: 5–7 days. Creator: 3–5 days. Elite: 24–48 hours. Add 2–3 days for shipping within the US, 5–10 days internationally.
