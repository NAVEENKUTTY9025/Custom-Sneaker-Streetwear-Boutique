OUR STORY

BORN FROM
THE STREETS.

SOLEHOUSE started as a small custom sneaker studio in 2018. What began as a passion project — painting kicks in a garage — grew into a global community of sneakerheads, artists, and streetwear lovers.

We don't just sell sneakers. We create wearable art, drop exclusive collections, and celebrate the culture that raised us.

EST. 2018 · CHICAGO
WHAT DRIVES US

The SOLEHOUSE Code

Three pillars that shape every pair we paint and every drop we release.

Creativity First

Every custom is a one-of-one canvas. We push boundaries, mix mediums, and never repeat a design.

Community Driven

We collaborate with local artists, support sneaker culture, and give back through mentorship programs.

Uncompromised Quality

Premium materials, archival paints, and a finish that lasts. If it's not perfect, it doesn't leave the studio.
